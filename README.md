## Licensing

VectorOPS requires an approved Operations License.

After installation:

1. Launch VectorOPS.
2. Select **Upgrade to Operations License**.
3. Enter your work email address.
4. Verify your email.
5. Your Operations License request will be reviewed by your Account Manager.
6. Once approved, restart VectorOPS.

VectorOPS performs a licensing handshake at startup and remains locked until an approved Operations License is confirmed.
